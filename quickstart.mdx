## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
You need only a modern web browser and an internet connection. No downloads or installations are required.
</Callout>

## Create your account

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign up">
Open your browser and go to the Canva homepage. Click the sign-up button, then create an account with your email address or a connected Google or Facebook profile.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete onboarding">
Answer the short prompts about your role and design goals. Canva uses this information to suggest relevant templates and features.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Start your first project

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a template">
From the home screen, browse categories or search for a template such as a social media graphic, presentation, or poster. Click Use this template to open it.
</Step>

<Step title="Edit your design">
Drag elements on the canvas to reposition them. Replace placeholder text and images with your own content using the toolbar on the left.
</Step>

<Step title="Export the finished design">
Click the Share button in the top right, select Download, and choose a format such as PNG, JPG, or PDF. Your file saves directly to your device.
</Step>
</Steps>
