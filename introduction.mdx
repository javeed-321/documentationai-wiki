## Welcome

Notion brings notes, docs, projects, and apps into one AI-powered workspace. You use intelligent agents, enterprise search, and meeting notes to keep work organized and moving.

It is designed to reduce tool sprawl, centralize knowledge, and help teams make decisions faster. Whether you are evaluating it for a small team or an enterprise, Notion gives you a single place to plan, write, automate, and collaborate.
