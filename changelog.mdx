<Update label="2024-10-15" description="v3.2.0" tags={["feature"]}>

  - Added Conversation Orchestrator for dynamic AI-driven routing across channels.

  - Introduced persistent Conversation Memory to retain context in multi-turn interactions.
</Update>

  - Enabled SIP trunking enhancements for improved call quality metrics.
</Update>

<Update label="2024-08-10" description="v3.0.0" tags={["feature"]}>
