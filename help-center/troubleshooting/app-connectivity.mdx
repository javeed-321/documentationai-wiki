## Common Issues

We know connectivity problems can stop you from working. The sections below list exact symptoms, likely causes, and step-by-step fixes.

<Expandable title="Desktop app crashes on launch" default-open="false">

The Slack desktop app quits right after opening and shows the message "Slack has stopped working."

This often happens when a corrupted cache file blocks the startup process.

1. Quit Slack completely from your task manager.
2. Open your file browser and go to the Slack cache folder.
3. Delete the contents of the `Cache` folder.
4. Restart the app and sign in again.

If the crash continues, reinstall the desktop app from the official download page.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Mobile push notifications stop arriving" default-open="false">

You stop receiving push alerts on your phone even though messages arrive on desktop.

Slack's mobile connection can drop after an OS update or when battery optimization blocks background activity.

1. Open your phone settings and find Slack in the app list.
2. Turn on "Allow background data" and disable any battery saver restrictions for Slack.
3. In the Slack app, go to Settings then Notifications and toggle the switches for the channels you need.
4. Force close and reopen the app to test.

Test by asking a teammate to send a direct message.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="App integrations fail to connect" default-open="false">

A connected app shows the error "Unable to reach integration server" when you try to use it.

The integration token may have expired or the workspace firewall blocks the connection.

1. Go to the workspace settings and select the Integrations tab.
2. Find the failing app and choose Reconnect.
3. When prompted, grant the requested permissions again.
4. Check that your network allows outbound connections on port 443.

If the error persists, remove the integration and add it fresh from the App Directory.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Network or proxy errors block sign-in" default-open="false">

The app displays "Proxy authentication failed" or "Network unreachable" during login.

Your corporate proxy or VPN may require extra configuration that Slack does not detect automatically.

1. Open Slack preferences and navigate to the Network section.
2. Select Manual proxy configuration.
3. Enter your proxy server address and port, then add your username and password if required.
4. Click Save and restart the app.

You can also run the command `slack --proxy http://proxy.example.com:8080` from the terminal to test the setting.

</Expandable>
