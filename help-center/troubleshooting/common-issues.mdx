## Common messenger problems

<Expandable title="Messenger does not load on your site" default-open="false">

The messenger fails to appear on your website pages.

**Cause**  
Your site blocks the Intercom script or the workspace ID is incorrect.

**Fix**  
1. Open your browser console and look for this error:  
   `Failed to load resource: the server responded with a status of 403`  
2. Confirm the Intercom script tag contains your exact workspace ID:  
   ```html
   <script>
     window.intercomSettings = { app_id: 'YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID' };
   </script>
   ```
3. Add `https://api.example.com` to your site's allowed script sources if you use a content security policy.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Help center articles do not appear in search" default-open="false">

Visitors see no results when they search the help center.

**Cause**  
Articles are not published or the search index is out of date.

**Fix**  
1. In Intercom, open each article and check that its status shows Published.  
2. Click the Reindex button on the Help Center settings page.  
3. Wait two minutes, then test a search for a known article title.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Chat notifications are not sent to teammates" default-open="false">

New visitor messages arrive in the inbox but no email or push alerts reach your team.

**Cause**  
Notification settings are turned off or the teammate email address is unverified.

**Fix**  
1. Go to Settings → Notifications and confirm Email and Mobile are enabled for the "New conversation" event.  
2. Ask each teammate to open their profile and verify the email address listed there.  
3. Send a test message from an incognito window and check the inbox for the notification.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="User identity fails to sync" default-open="false">

User details such as name or email do not update in Intercom after login.

**Cause**  
The `user_id` or `email` value sent to Intercom does not match the value used on first contact.

**Fix**  
1. In your code, ensure you call `Intercom('boot', { user_id: '12345', email: 'user@example.com' });` on every page after login.  
2. Check the browser console for this message:  
   `Intercom: user_id mismatch detected`  
3. Update the value on your site so it exactly matches the original identifier.

</Expandable>
