## Common Issues

We know how frustrating it feels when Slack stops working the way you expect. The sections below walk through frequent problems with clear causes and steps to fix them.

<Expandable title="Cannot connect or sign in" default-open="false">
**Problem**

You see the error `Unable to connect to Slack. Check your internet connection.` or the login screen loops without success.

**Cause**

Your network blocks Slack domains or your session token has expired.

**Fix**

1. Open a browser and visit `https://app.example.com` to confirm internet access.
2. Sign out completely, then sign back in.
3. If the issue continues, ask your network admin to allow traffic to `*.slack.com` and `*.example.com`.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Notifications never arrive" default-open="false">
**Problem**

Messages appear in the channel but you receive no desktop, mobile, or email alerts.

**Cause**

Notification settings are turned off or your device blocks Slack from sending alerts.

**Fix**

- Go to **Preferences > Notifications** and enable `All new messages`.
- On your phone, check that Slack has permission in system settings.
- Test by sending yourself a direct message from another account.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Messages fail to send or deliver" default-open="false">
**Problem**

You type a message, press enter, and see `Message not sent. Tap to retry.` The message stays stuck.

**Cause**

A temporary server hiccup or a file attachment that exceeds size limits.

**Fix**

- Click the retry button once.
- Remove any large files and try sending text only.
- If the error repeats, check your connection speed and restart the app.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Apps or integrations stop working" default-open="false">
**Problem**

An app shows the error `App failed to respond` or stops posting updates to the channel.

**Cause**

The app's authentication token expired or the workspace admin revoked access.

**Fix**

1. Open the app settings from the channel header.
2. Click **Reconnect** and follow the OAuth prompts.
3. Ask a workspace owner to review permissions under **Settings & administration > Manage apps**.
</Expandable>

If none of these steps solve the problem, reach out to Slack support with the exact error message you see.
