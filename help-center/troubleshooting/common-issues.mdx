## Message Delivery Issues

<Expandable title="Message not sending" default-open="false">

Your message stays stuck with the status "Sending..." and never reaches the channel.

This happens when your connection drops mid-send or Slack hits a temporary rate limit on your workspace.

Check your internet connection first. Then try sending a short test message in a direct message to yourself. If it fails, restart the Slack app and sign back in.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Messages appear only after refresh" default-open="false">

Sent messages vanish until you manually refresh the channel view.

A sync delay between your client and Slack servers often causes this, especially on slower networks.

Open the channel, then run the keyboard shortcut `Cmd/Ctrl + R` to force a refresh. If the issue repeats, sign out and sign back in from the account menu.

</Expandable>

## Notification Problems

<Expandable title="No notifications for new messages" default-open="false">

You see the red badge on the workspace icon but hear no sound or see no desktop alert.

Slack may have lost permission to show notifications, or your Do Not Disturb schedule is active.

Go to Preferences > Notifications and confirm "Allow notifications" is on. Check your system settings to grant Slack permission to send alerts.

</Expandable>

## Workspace Sync Issues

<Expandable title="Workspace fails to load channels" default-open="false">

The sidebar shows "Syncing..." for minutes, then displays an error reading "Unable to load channels."

This usually stems from a corrupted local cache after an interrupted update.

Quit Slack completely. On macOS, delete the folder `~/Library/Application Support/Slack` and reopen the app. It will rebuild the cache on next sign-in.

</Expandable>

## Login and SSO Errors

<Expandable title="SSO login loop" default-open="false">

You enter your company credentials, get redirected, then land back at the login screen with the message "Authentication failed. Please try again."

The identity provider may have an expired session token or a mismatched redirect URL.

Clear your browser cookies for both Slack and your SSO provider. Then start the login flow again from the desktop app.

</Expandable>
