You can reach out for help in a few different ways. Choose the path that matches what you need right now.

## Opening a support ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">

Sign in to your workspace, click the question mark icon in the bottom left, then choose "Contact support." Fill out the form with a short description of what is happening and submit.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">

Add the exact steps you took, any error messages you see, and the browser or device you use. Screenshots help the team understand the issue faster.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Accessing the Intercom community

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I find the Intercom community?" default-open="true">

From the help menu in your workspace, select "Community." You can browse discussions, search past answers, and post your own question.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do I need an account to read community posts?">

No. Anyone can read existing threads. You only need to sign in if you want to post or reply.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Requesting a product demo

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I request a guided onboarding session?" default-open="true">

Go to the help menu and pick "Request a demo." Choose a time that works for you and share a brief note about your team size and goals.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Is the demo session free?">

Yes. The session is free and lasts about 30 minutes. A specialist walks you through the features that fit your needs.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
