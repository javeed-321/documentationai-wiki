## Billing Inquiries

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I update my payment method?" default-open="true">

Open your workspace settings, select Billing, then edit the card on file. Changes take effect immediately for the next cycle.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Where can I find past invoices?">

Invoices appear in the Billing section under workspace settings. Download them as PDFs for your records.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enterprise Support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I reach dedicated enterprise support?" default-open="true">

Enterprise customers receive a dedicated support contact in their account portal. Submit a ticket there for priority handling.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What does enterprise support cover?">

It includes 24/7 assistance for compliance, security, and large-scale workspace issues.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Feature Requests

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I suggest a new feature?" default-open="true">

Use the feedback form inside the app or visit the help center feedback page. Our product team reviews every submission.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="When will my requested feature be added?">

We prioritize based on user impact. Check the release notes regularly for updates on popular requests.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Account Recovery

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What if I forgot my password?" default-open="true">

Click Forgot password on the sign-in screen. We send a reset link to your registered email within minutes.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I recover a deactivated account?">

Contact support through the help center with your workspace URL. We verify ownership before reactivation.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
