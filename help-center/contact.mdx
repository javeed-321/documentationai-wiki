## Chat and Ticket Support

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I start a live chat with support?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Shopify admin, click the help icon in the bottom right, and select Chat. Available agents respond quickly during business hours.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How long does a support ticket take to resolve?">
    Most tickets receive a first reply within 24 hours. Add clear details and screenshots to help the team assist you faster.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I request a callback instead of waiting online?">
    Yes. From the help menu choose Request a call and enter your phone number. A support specialist will reach you shortly.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other merchants for advice?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Shopify Community forums directly from the help menu. Search existing threads first, then post your question with relevant tags.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are the forums moderated by Shopify staff?">
    Shopify team members and experienced merchants monitor the forums. They often reply to common questions within a day.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Partner and Expert Help

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I find a Shopify partner for custom work?" default-open="true">
    Go to the Shopify Experts marketplace inside your admin. Filter by skill, location, or budget and send a request for quotes.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What services do Shopify partners typically offer?">
    Partners help with store setup, theme customization, app development, and marketing. Review their portfolios before hiring.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Help Center Search Tips

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How can I find answers faster in the help center?" default-open="true">
    Use short, specific phrases such as "change order status" instead of long sentences. Add your plan name for more targeted results.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if the search results do not match my question?">
    Try the filters on the left to narrow by topic or date. You can also browse the category list at the bottom of the help center home.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
