## Submitting Support Tickets

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Zendesk account, go to the Help Center admin, and select Submit a request. Provide details about your issue so the team can assist quickly.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What information should I include in my ticket?">
    Include your account email, the exact steps that led to the problem, any error messages, and screenshots if possible. This helps resolve your issue faster.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How long does it take to get a reply?">
    Response times vary by plan and issue severity. You will receive an initial confirmation within minutes, followed by a detailed reply from the support team.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community and Developer Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask questions in the community?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Zendesk Community forums directly from the Help Center. Search existing threads first, then post your question with clear details for quick answers from other users.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are there forums for developers?">
    Yes. The developer community section covers API usage, custom integrations, and theme development. Share code snippets and get feedback from experienced users.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Status Page and Incident Reporting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where do I check Zendesk service status?" default-open="true">
    Go to the official Zendesk status page linked from the Help Center footer. It shows real-time uptime for all services and any ongoing incidents.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How are incidents reported and tracked?">
    Major incidents appear on the status page with updates. You can also subscribe to notifications so you receive alerts about resolved or new issues.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Professional Services Options

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What professional services does Zendesk offer?" default-open="true">
    Zendesk provides implementation help, custom theme development, and training sessions. Contact your account manager or open a ticket to discuss your needs.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I request a consultation?">
    Submit a ticket with the subject Professional Services Request and describe your project goals. A specialist will reach out to schedule a call.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
