## Submitting a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your workspace, go to the Help menu, and select Contact Support. Fill out the form with a clear description of your issue.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details help my ticket get answered faster?">
    Include your workspace URL, the exact steps that led to the problem, any error messages, and screenshots if possible.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How long until I hear back?">
    Most tickets receive a first reply within one business day. Urgent issues marked as blocking work are prioritized.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other Slack users for help?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Slack Community at community.slack.com. Search existing threads or post your question in the right channel.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are the forums moderated?">
    Yes. Slack staff and experienced community members keep discussions helpful and on topic.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enterprise Support Options

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What extra support comes with Slack Enterprise?" default-open="true">
    Enterprise plans include a dedicated support team, faster response times, and a named account manager.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can my team request a call with support?">
    Yes. Enterprise customers can schedule a live session when the issue requires deeper troubleshooting.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Feedback Channels

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I share product feedback?" default-open="true">
    Use the Feedback option inside Slack or visit the feedback portal linked from the Help menu. Your ideas go directly to the product team.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I suggest new features?">
    Absolutely. Feature requests are reviewed regularly and help shape future updates.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
