## Submitting Support Tickets

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">

Sign in to your Help Center, then click the Submit a request button. Choose the category that matches your issue and add as much detail as you can. You will receive an email confirmation with your ticket number.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include?">

Describe the exact steps you took, any error messages you saw, and the browser or device you used. Attach screenshots when they help explain the problem.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums Access

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I find the community forums?" default-open="true">

From the Help Center footer, click Community. Search existing threads first. If you do not find an answer, post your question with clear steps and any relevant screenshots.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How quickly do community members reply?">

Active community members usually answer within a few hours during business days. Zendesk staff also monitor popular threads and step in when needed.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Live Chat Options

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Is live chat available?" default-open="true">

Live chat appears in the lower-right corner of your Help Center when an agent is online. Click the chat icon, describe your issue briefly, and an agent will join the conversation.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if chat is offline?">

You can still submit a ticket. The next available agent will follow up by email and continue the conversation in the ticket thread.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Onboarding and Training

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I start onboarding?" default-open="true">

After you sign up, open the Getting Started checklist inside your account. Complete the short videos and tasks in order to set up your first Help Center pages.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Where can I find self-paced training?">

Visit the Training section in the Help Center. You will find free courses on article writing, workflow setup, and reporting. Certificates are available for completed courses.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enterprise SLA Support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What support level comes with Enterprise plans?" default-open="true">

Enterprise plans include a dedicated support contact, 24/7 phone access, and a one-hour response target for urgent issues. Your account manager schedules a quarterly review to discuss ongoing needs.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I request priority handling?">

Add the word URGENT to the subject line of your ticket and reference your SLA agreement. The support team routes these tickets to the on-call specialist immediately.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
