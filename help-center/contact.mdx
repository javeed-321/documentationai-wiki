## Opening a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I submit a ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Help Center, click the question mark icon, and select Submit a request. Fill in the form with a clear description of your issue and any relevant screenshots.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details should I include?">
    Provide your account email, the exact steps that led to the problem, and any error messages you see. This helps our team respond faster.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority Support Plans

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I access priority support?" default-open="true">
    Upgrade your plan in Account settings under Billing. Priority plans include faster response times and dedicated support contacts.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What response times apply?">
    Priority tickets receive a reply within one hour during business hours. Standard plans aim for four hours.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Using In-App Chat

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I start a live chat?" default-open="true">
    While signed in, click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of the Help Center. Available agents appear during support hours.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Is chat available 24/7?">
    Chat operates during your selected time zone business hours. Outside those windows, submit a ticket for next-day follow-up.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Escalating Critical Outages

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I report a service outage?" default-open="true">
    Use the priority ticket form and select Critical outage. Include your account ID and the affected feature so we can investigate immediately.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I request a phone callback?">
    Yes. After opening the ticket, reply with your phone number and preferred time. Priority plan customers receive callbacks within 30 minutes.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
