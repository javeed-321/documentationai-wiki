## Support Channels

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How can I reach Intercom support?" default-open="true">

You can start a conversation directly from your Help Center dashboard using the in-app messenger. Support teams respond within one business day.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details help speed up my request?">

Include your workspace ID, a short description of the issue, and any screenshots. This helps the team assist you faster.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forum

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I ask questions from other users?" default-open="true">

Visit the Intercom community forum to browse threads and post your own questions. Many common Help Center issues already have detailed answers there.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I search existing answers?">

Use the forum search bar with keywords from your question. Filter by topic tags such as Help Center or billing to narrow results quickly.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Billing Inquiries

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Who handles billing questions?" default-open="true">

Send billing questions through the in-app messenger and select the billing topic. The finance team reviews requests within two business days.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I update my payment method myself?">

Yes. Go to workspace settings, select Billing, and enter new card details. Changes take effect on your next invoice date.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Feature Requests

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I suggest a new Help Center feature?" default-open="true">

Open the messenger, choose Feature Request, and describe what would help your workflow. The product team reviews every submission.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I hear back about my idea?">

You receive an update when the request moves into review or ships. Popular ideas often appear in the public roadmap within a few weeks.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
