## Creating a workspace

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create a new workspace?" default-open="true">

Sign in to Slack on the web or desktop app. Click your workspace name in the top left, then choose Create a new workspace. Enter your email address, pick a name for the workspace, and follow the prompts to invite teammates.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I create more than one workspace?">

Yes. You can create additional workspaces from the same account. Each workspace keeps its own channels and members separate.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Joining channels

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I join a channel?" default-open="true">

In the sidebar, click Channels, then Browse channels. Search or scroll to find the channel you want, then click Join.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if I cannot find the channel I need?">

Ask a teammate to invite you or check with your workspace admin. Some channels are private and require an invitation.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Sending DMs

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I send a direct message?" default-open="true">

Click the plus icon next to Direct messages in the sidebar. Start typing a teammate's name, select them, then type your message and press Enter.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I message someone outside my workspace?">

Direct messages stay inside your workspace. To reach people at other companies, ask them to join your workspace or use a shared channel if one exists.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Mobile app setup

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I set up the Slack app on my phone?" default-open="true">

Download Slack from the App Store or Google Play. Open the app, enter your workspace URL or sign in with your email, then follow the prompts to verify your account.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Why am I not receiving notifications on mobile?">

Open the app settings and check that notifications are turned on for your device. Make sure Slack has permission in your phone's notification settings as well.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
