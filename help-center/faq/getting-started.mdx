## Setting up your workspace

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create a new workspace?" default-open="true">
    Go to slack.com and click Create Workspace. Enter your email address and follow the prompts to name your workspace. You will receive a confirmation email to complete setup.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I use an existing email domain for my workspace?">
    Yes. During creation Slack checks your domain and offers to connect it if available. This lets teammates join with matching email addresses automatically.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Inviting teammates

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I invite people to join my workspace?" default-open="true">
    Click the workspace name in the top left and select Invite people. Enter email addresses or share the invite link. You can set different permission levels for new members.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens if someone does not have a Slack account?">
    They receive an email invitation. After they accept and create a free account they are added to your workspace automatically.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Channel basics

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What is the difference between public and private channels?" default-open="true">
    Public channels appear in search and anyone in the workspace can join. Private channels require an invitation and stay hidden from non-members.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I send my first message?">
    Open any channel and type in the message box at the bottom. Press Enter to send. You can also reply to specific messages by hovering and clicking the reply arrow.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Mobile versus desktop

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I use Slack on my phone and computer at the same time?" default-open="true">
    Yes. Sign in with the same account on both devices. Messages sync across all platforms so you stay up to date wherever you work.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Which features work differently on mobile?">
    The mobile app supports most desktop features but offers push notifications and quick emoji reactions. Some advanced admin tools remain available only on desktop.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
