## Account setup

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create my first Help Center?" default-open="true">

  Go to your workspace settings and select Help Center. Click the create button, choose a name, and pick a subdomain. Intercom sets up the basic structure for you right away.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if my domain is already in use?">

  Choose a unique subdomain during setup. You can connect a custom domain later in the branding section once the Help Center is live.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Article creation

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I write and format my first article?" default-open="true">

  Open the content tab and select New article. Use the editor to add headings, lists, and images. Keep sentences short so readers find answers quickly.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I reuse content across articles?">

  Yes. Create reusable snippets in the content library and insert them anywhere. Changes to the snippet update everywhere automatically.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Permissions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Who can edit articles by default?" default-open="true">

  Team members with admin or content manager roles can create and edit articles. You can invite teammates and assign roles from the team settings page.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I limit who publishes content?">

  Set publishing permissions to specific roles only. Regular team members can draft articles while only managers approve and publish them.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Publishing workflow

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What happens when I publish an article?" default-open="true">

  The article becomes visible in your Help Center immediately. Visitors see the latest version and you can track views from the analytics dashboard.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I schedule articles to publish later?">

  Draft the article first, then use the schedule option in the publish menu. Pick a date and time and Intercom handles the rest.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
