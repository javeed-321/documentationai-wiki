## Account creation and plans

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create a Shopify account?" default-open="true">

Go to the Shopify website and click the start free trial button. Enter your email address, store name, and password. Shopify guides you through the first steps right away.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Which plan should I choose to start?">

The Basic plan works well for most new stores. You can upgrade later as your sales grow. All plans include a free trial so you can test before paying.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Adding your first products

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add products to my store?" default-open="true">

From your Shopify admin, go to Products and click Add product. Fill in the title, description, price, and upload photos. Save the product to make it live on your store.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I add product variants like sizes or colors?">

Yes. On the product page, click the Variants section and enter the options you need. Shopify creates separate listings for each combination automatically.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Connecting a domain

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I connect my own domain name?" default-open="true">

In your Shopify admin, go to Settings then Domains. Click Connect existing domain and enter the domain you own. Follow the prompts to update your DNS records at your domain registrar.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if I do not have a domain yet?">

Shopify lets you buy a domain directly inside the admin. Search for an available name, add it to your cart, and complete checkout. Shopify sets everything up for you.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enabling payments

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I start accepting payments?" default-open="true">

Go to Settings then Payments. Choose Shopify Payments to get started quickly, or connect another provider such as PayPal. Enter your business details and bank account to finish setup.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do I need to verify my identity first?">

Yes. Shopify asks for basic business information and ID verification to meet payment regulations. This step keeps your store secure and helps funds reach your account on time.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
