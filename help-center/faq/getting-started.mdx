## Installing the Messenger

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add the messenger to my site?" default-open="true">

Copy the code snippet from your Intercom settings and paste it into the head of every page. Refresh your site to see the messenger appear in the corner.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="The messenger does not show up after install">

Check that the code is on every page and that your domain matches the one listed in settings. Clear your browser cache and try again.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Creating Your First Article

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where do I write my first help article?" default-open="true">

Go to the Articles section, click New article, add a clear title and body, then publish. Your article appears in the messenger right away.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I preview the article before publishing?">

Yes. Use the preview button in the editor to see exactly how it looks inside the messenger.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Configuring Team Inboxes

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I set up an inbox for my support team?" default-open="true">

Create a new inbox from the Inbox settings, add team members, and assign conversation rules. Messages now route to the right people.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to old conversations when I add new teammates?">

Existing conversations stay in their current inbox. New teammates only see conversations assigned after they join.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Setting Up Proactive Messages

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I send a message before a visitor asks?" default-open="true">

Create a new message in the Series section, pick the visitor segment, write your note, and set it to show on page load or after a delay.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I test the message on myself first?">

Yes. Use the test mode toggle to see the message exactly as your visitors will.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
