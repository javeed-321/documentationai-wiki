## Initial account setup

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create my first Zendesk Help Center account?" default-open="true">
    Visit the sign-up page at example.com and enter your email. Confirm the account through the link sent to your inbox, then choose a subdomain for your help center.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details do I need during setup?">
    Provide your company name, support email, and time zone. These choices shape the default branding and notification settings.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Importing existing content

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I bring over articles from another system?" default-open="true">
    Yes. Use the import tool in the admin panel. Export your old content as CSV or HTML, then upload the file. Zendesk maps the fields automatically.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens to images and attachments during import?">
    Images and files are copied into the new help center. Check the import summary for any missing items and re-upload them manually if needed.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enabling self-service portals

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I turn on the self-service portal for customers?" default-open="true">
    Go to Settings, then Channels, and enable the Help Center option. Publish the portal and share the link with your customers.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I restrict who sees the portal?">
    Yes. Set visibility to registered users only or add password protection in the access settings.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Agent workspace integration

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How does the help center connect to the agent workspace?" default-open="true">
    Link the two inside the admin area under Integrations. Agents then see relevant articles while replying to tickets.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if agents cannot find suggested articles?">
    Make sure your articles have clear titles and tags. Test suggestions by opening a sample ticket in the workspace.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Mobile help center access

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do customers reach the help center on their phones?" default-open="true">
    The help center works in any mobile browser. Add a link to your app or website footer so users can open it with one tap.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Is there a dedicated mobile app for the help center?">
    No separate app is required. The responsive design adjusts automatically to smaller screens.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
