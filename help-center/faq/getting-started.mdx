## Account setup and branding

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create my first Help Center?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Zendesk account and navigate to the Help Center section. Click the option to create a new site, then follow the prompts to name it and select your support team.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I customize the look of my Help Center?">
    Yes. Go to the branding settings and upload your logo, choose colors that match your company style, and adjust the layout to fit your needs.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Publishing your first articles

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I add my first article?" default-open="true">
    From the content dashboard, select Articles and click New Article. Write your content, add any images or links, then hit publish to make it visible to users.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens if I need to update an article later?">
    Open the article from your list, make your changes, and save. Published updates appear right away for your customers.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Managing user permissions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Who can edit Help Center content?" default-open="true">
    You control access in the team settings. Assign roles like admin or contributor to let specific people create or update articles without giving them full account access.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I keep some articles private?">
    Use the visibility options when publishing. Set articles to internal only so only your team can see them.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Integrating with Zendesk Support

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How does Help Center connect to my support tickets?" default-open="true">
    Enable the integration in your Zendesk Support settings. This lets customers search the Help Center before submitting a ticket and links related articles to open requests.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if my team uses different tools alongside Zendesk?">
    The Help Center works with Zendesk Support out of the box. Check the integrations panel if you need to connect additional systems later.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
