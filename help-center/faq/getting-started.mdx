## Account and workspace setup

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my first workspace?" default-open="true">

Sign in with your email, confirm your address, then name your workspace. Zendesk creates a starter Help Center automatically so you can begin adding content right away.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I invite teammates during setup?">

Yes. After the workspace loads, open Team settings and enter their email addresses. They receive an invitation link and join with the role you assign.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Theme and branding options

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I match my brand colors?" default-open="true">

Go to Appearance, open the Theme editor, and pick your primary and accent colors. Changes appear live in the preview so you can adjust before publishing.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I upload my own logo and favicon?">

Yes. Upload your logo and favicon files in the same Appearance panel. Zendesk resizes them automatically for headers and browser tabs.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Article permissions and visibility

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Who can see my articles by default?" default-open="true">

New articles stay private until you publish them. Only signed-in agents can view drafts; everyone else sees only published content.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I hide an article from search?">

Edit the article, open the Visibility menu, and choose Internal. The article stays published for agents but disappears from public search results.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enabling customer accounts

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Do customers need accounts to read articles?" default-open="true">

No. Public articles are visible to anyone. Accounts become useful only when you want customers to submit tickets or view private content.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I let customers create their own accounts?">

Turn on self-service registration in Customer settings. Customers can then sign up with email and immediately access any content you have marked for registered users.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Mobile and multilingual support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Does the Help Center work on phones?" default-open="true">

Yes. The default theme is responsive and adjusts automatically. You can also install the free mobile app so agents can answer questions from their phones.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I offer the site in multiple languages?">

Add languages in the Localization panel, then translate each article. Visitors see the version that matches their browser language.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
